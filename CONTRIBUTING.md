# Contributing

termchat is licensed under AGPL-3.0. The maintainer may also offer it under other licenses, for example for a hosted service or commercial use.

To keep that possible, contributions require a copyright assignment or a contributor license agreement (CLA) that lets the maintainer relicense your work. Open an issue or pull request to discuss your change first, and the maintainer will arrange the paperwork before merging.

Run `go test -race ./...` before you submit.
